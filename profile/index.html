DUANGPAWAN LUANGUTHAI

GRAPHIC DESIGNER

TN ,Duangpawan Luanguthai, was born and raise in Bangkok, Thailand since 25 April 2005. She have educated in Bangkok until the end of her High school. In 2022 she relocated to London for the UK foundation for Art and Design program. Currently she is a sophmore student at Parsons majoring in Communication Design.

She’s a passionate and visionary designer who has an innate ability to spot great design and transform the impossible into reality. With a sharp eye for aesthetics and a knack for innovation, she bring fresh, bold ideas to life, crafting designs that captivate and inspire. Fueled by creativity and a relentless drive to push boundaries, TN thrives in dynamic environments, turning challenges into opportunities and concepts into stunning visual experiences. Always exploring new possibilities, she is ready to collaborate and create something extraordinary.

Outside of work, She loves exploring the city, finding inspiration in everyday life, and connecting with a diverse range of people. Whether discovering hidden urban gems or immersing in nature and culture, she constantly seek new experiences that fuel creativity and perspective.
